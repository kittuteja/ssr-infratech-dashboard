How amounts, advances and refunds are counted

This module records transactions only. It does not move money. Pending and void payments never count in cash totals. Create an amount due separately, then apply one or more completed payments to it.

Advances count as cash when completed. They reduce a due only after you apply them. Refunds are linked payments in the opposite direction and are included in that direction’s cash total. Reverse any affected application before refunding money; the amount due reopens.

Payments use their payment date. Dues use their due date for the selected range; their balance is calculated through the selected end date. Future dues need a future end date. Corrections restate reports using the latest version; history retains every previous version. Application and reversal dates use today in India Standard Time.

All changes require an SSR Admin account. A person’s business roles do not create a login or grant access. Deactivation preserves their full history.
